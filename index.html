About Simmi

Simmi is a browser-based voice assistant interface. It can listen to voice commands, speak responses, open supported websites, tell time/date and use your device location when permission is granted.

SIMMI CAPABILITIES

Smart Features

🎙️ Voice Recognition

Tap the main power button and speak a command. Supported browsers can convert your voice to text.

🔊 Voice Response

Simmi can speak its response using the device's built-in speech synthesis.

📍 Location

When you allow permission, Simmi can display your current latitude and longitude.

🌐 Web Actions

Commands such as Google, YouTube and websites can be opened from the assistant.

⚡ Quick Commands

Use the predefined buttons or type your own supported command.
